# Charlie Hills's Claude Motion brief

**Creator:** [Charlie Hills](https://charliehills.substack.com/)

**Original publication:** [Claude Motion: make motion graphics](https://charliehills.substack.com/p/claude-motion-graphics). This page summarizes the publicly visible starter brief; read the publication for the creator's wording.

Hills's public example asks for a launch video using a supplied product announcement. The brief specifies duration, a destination platform, words that communicate while the video is muted, and an ending call to action. It restricts the content to the announcement rather than asking the agent to invent product claims.

## What the brief teaches

- **Supply the message.** Give the agent the announcement and exact call to action before it plans the visuals.
- **Name the viewing context.** A muted social feed makes legible on-screen copy part of the brief.
- **Set the scope.** Duration and destination help constrain the amount of information in the video.

The article describes a workflow in the Claude app and website. It does not establish reusable source code. The remainder of the article requires a subscription; this summary does not claim access to it or a playback review.
