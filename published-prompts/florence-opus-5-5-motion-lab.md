# Florence's Opus 5.5 Motion Lab prompts

**Creator:** [Florence (`@flxrnc`)](https://x.com/flxrnc)  
**Original publication:** [*Opus 5.5 Motion Lab: Make Claude Animate Like a Studio*](https://x.com/i/article/2105052379590578176), shared in [this post](https://x.com/flxrnc/status/2105311836190978392) on September 30, 2026.

The article prints the initial prompts and subsequent instructions for three code-rendered motion pieces. Its earbuds comparison has two separate visual starting prompts. Open the article to read or copy the creator's exact text; the descriptions below are original summaries.

## Visual starting prompts

| Find in the article | What the prompt directs | Published visual follow-ups |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening shot, before “Experiment 1”: **Liquid Chrome** prompt | A six-second, square, seamless title loop using three.js, a raymarched liquid form, letter geometry, numbered choreography, lights, and a moving camera. | 10 notes covering reflections, glints, letter spacing, droplets leaving frame, shape continuity, and the return to the opening blob. |
| “Experiment 1: Vague prompt vs directed prompt”: **Prompt A** | A short request for a premium, modern 3D wireless-earbuds animation in one HTML file. | None; the author deliberately kept the first result. |
| Same section: **Prompt B** | The same product and output format, with a goal, timed shots, material and lighting values, camera positions, motion behavior, and excluded default effects. | 15 notes across four review rounds, adjusting product modeling, exposure, reflections, camera movement, and a beat-frame hold. |
| “Experiment 2: Five animation principles on a 3D mascot”: **The exact prompt** | An eight-second square loop with a voxel mascot, a timeline floor, labeled principles, specified poses and landing frames, lighting, and a seamless return. | Four notes correcting color, shape, timing, and framing. |

The article reports 29 visual follow-ups in total. The earbuds comparison is a workflow illustration: the directed side also had 15 revisions and more time, so it does not isolate the effect of the starting prompt.

The article reports 17 sound follow-ups in total. Together with the four visual and three sound starting prompts, it documents **53 production prompts and follow-ups**. It also supplies a general prompt template and troubleshooting instructions outside this count.

## Reusable prompt structure

The article's “Anatomy of a directed motion prompt” section provides a field-by-field template: intended takeaway, output, duration and frame rate, visual style, shots, motion, type, palette, sound, exclusions, and render review. Its “The lab loop: render, look, fix” section shows how follow-up instructions name exact frames and visible defects. Its “When an experiment breaks” table provides targeted repair instructions.

The creator's numbers and follow-up counts are reported from the article. They are useful examples, not universal timing rules or independently verified performance claims. The article includes the rendered examples, but this entry catalogs their published prompts; it is not a playback review.
