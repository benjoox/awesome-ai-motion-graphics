# Code of conduct

Keep participation respectful, constructive, and welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

Discuss ideas and work rather than attacking people. Give specific feedback and credit creators. Do not harass, discriminate, publish private information, spam, or misrepresent another person's work.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, close inappropriate submissions, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate and consistent.

For abuse or sensitive personal matters, use [GitHub's abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Do not publish personal information or detailed accusations in an issue. Ordinary editorial disagreements belong in the relevant issue or PR.
