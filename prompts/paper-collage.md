# Paper collage

**Reference:** [Mid-Autumn paper collage short](https://claudevideo.org/videos/paper-collage-cat-on-a-moonlit-rooftop-for-mid-autumn) — visual reference; the creator did not publish a prompt.

```text
Create a [30]-second paper-collage short about [subject or story]. Use the supplied licensed images and textures: [assets], or draw original pieces in code. Do not reuse another creator's character, composition, or cultural imagery without a reason tied to this story.

Art direction: [paper types], [limited palette], [grain or print treatment], and [light direction]. Build depth with foreground, middle ground, and background layers. Give each cut-out a believable pivot or slide path, with small texture variation that does not jitter. Design three scenes with a visual link between them, such as a shape passed from one scene to the next.

Provide a storyboard and asset list first. Set the stage to [size] at [fps]. Animate with explicit time, render keyframes and a preview, then fix layer collisions, unreadable type, and abrupt transitions before export.
```
