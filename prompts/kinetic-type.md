# Kinetic typography

**Reference:** [Chapter-titled kinetic type reel](https://claudevideo.org/videos/chapter-titled-kinetic-type-reel-on-indian-civilization) — visual reference; the creator's prompt may not be available.

```text
Make a [20]-second kinetic typography piece about [message]. The spoken or on-screen words are: [paste exact copy]. Preserve this wording. The audience should remember [one takeaway].

Use a fixed [aspect ratio] stage and [2–3]-color palette. Choose one type family and establish a hierarchy for headline, supporting words, and small labels. Group the copy into [number] phrases. Assign each phrase a time range and one motion idea related to its meaning: reveal, compress, stretch, stack, scatter, or align. Let one phrase finish before the next takes focus.

If music is supplied, measure its beat grid and place major changes on beats; otherwise use a clear internal rhythm without assuming a BPM. Keep every phrase readable long enough to speak aloud. Avoid decorative movement that hides letters.

Implement as seekable animation: the frame at time t must depend only on t and the supplied inputs. Deliver the source, a beat sheet, and an exported [MP4/WebM].
```
