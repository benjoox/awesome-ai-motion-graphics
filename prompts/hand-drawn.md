# Hand-drawn animation

**Reference:** [Doodle history animation](https://claudevideo.org/videos/doodle-animation-traces-humanitys-history-to-ais-birth) — creator example with a published prompt.

```text
Create a [40]-second hand-drawn animation explaining [story or concept]. Draw original characters and props based on this brief: [design notes]. Keep a model sheet with silhouettes, proportions, colors, and key expressions so the subject remains recognizable from shot to shot.

Use a restrained hand-drawn look: [brush, paper, line weight]. Storyboard [number] scenes, each with one action and a clear transition. Animate poses and spacing first, then add limited line variation and texture. Keep deliberate holds where the audience needs to read or understand a gesture. Avoid random per-frame noise that makes the image flicker.

Work on a fixed [size] stage at [fps]. Make rendering reproducible by seeding any procedural texture and deriving poses from time. Check continuity and character proportions at scene boundaries, then export the video and source.
```
