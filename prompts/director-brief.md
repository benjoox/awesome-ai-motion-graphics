# Director's brief

**References:** [Donald's brief](https://x.com/donaldjewkes/status/2102801469976248500) and [Pradeep's template](https://x.com/pradeepXkapoor/status/2103177003885273599). This is an original template.

~~~text
Direct a [duration]-second film about [subject] for [audience]. The viewer should understand or feel [outcome]. The premise is [one sentence], ending [payoff], and tone [direction].

Inputs: [sources], [exact copy], [assets and provenance], [references], [audio], [renderer], [formats and fps]. Preserve facts and wording. State consequential missing inputs. Paid providers need [authorized budget]; reference credential variable names only.

Write a timed beat sheet. Specify each shot's purpose, visual state, focal subject, action, transition, copy, and sound. For recurring characters, define a model sheet and continuity rules. Choose holds and payoffs for this story rather than imposing a cut on every beat.

Work through plan, representative stills, animatic with sound, animation, review, and export. Preserve requested approval gates [gates]. Derive frames from time and inputs. Archive a working version before major revisions.

Inspect the render, identify three consequential defects with timestamps, and correct them. Deliver video, source, asset credits, render command, representative frames, and limitations.
~~~
