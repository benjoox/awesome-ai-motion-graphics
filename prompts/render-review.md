# Render review

**References:** [Joseph Mables's iteration account](https://x.com/mablesjoseph/status/2103465246014746943) and [Movez's course](https://x.com/0xMovez/status/2104216919033192746). This is an original template.

~~~text
Review [render and source paths] against [brief]. Inspect contact sheets, phone-size samples, consecutive frames around transitions, and playback with sound when available. State which evidence you inspected.

Check hook, comprehension, reading time, composition, continuity, motion, variety, brand accuracy, and sound alignment. For each material defect give timestamp or frame, symptom, effect on the viewer, and a concrete correction.

Rank findings: blocking defects such as false claims, missing content, clipping, or failed output; comprehension and continuity problems; then optional polish. Select three highest-impact fixes. Preserve accurate copy and the approved direction.

Re-render affected intervals and review again. Do not infer audio alignment from stills or manufacture a passing self-score. Finish when material defects are resolved and the brief is met, or explain the remaining limitation.
~~~
