# 3D scene

**Reference:** [Glowing lava-planet scene](https://claudevideo.org/videos/a-glowing-cracked-lava-planet-sphere-hangs-in-space) — creator example with a published prompt.

```text
Build a [15]-second cinematic 3D reveal of [subject]. The viewer must notice [one defining feature] first. Visual direction: [material, palette, lighting, environment]. Camera path: [starting view] to [ending view]. Avoid adding objects that distract from the subject.

Start with a simple scene plan: geometry, materials, lights, camera keyframes, and one secondary motion system. Use [Three.js/WebGL/Blender] and a fixed [size] frame at [fps]. Keep the subject readable as a silhouette before adding glow, particles, or post effects. If textures or models are supplied, use only those assets and record their licenses; otherwise build original geometry and procedural materials.

Make camera and object poses functions of time so a frame can be rendered without playing earlier frames. Check the first, middle, and final frames for clipping, depth confusion, and excessive bloom. Deliver source and an exported video.
```
