# Motion design showreel

**Reference:** [Receding grid showreel](https://claudevideo.org/videos/showreel-scene-renders-a-receding-grid-with-red-dots) — creator example with a published prompt.

```text
Create a [15]-second motion design showreel for [designer or studio]. Make it a sequence of three distinct studies, not a montage of unrelated effects. The through-line is [shape, word, or motif]; carry it through every transition.

Stage: [1920×1080 or 1080×1920], [fps] fps. Palette: [colors]. Typography: [font or characteristics]. Audience: [who will watch]. The final card must read exactly: [name and call to action].

Plan a timed beat sheet before coding: opening signature (0–3s), geometric motion (3–7s), type or interface transformation (7–12s), and final identity (12–15s). Give each study one clear focal point. Use scale, spacing, and motion curves deliberately; make text legible on a phone.

Build with [HTML/SVG/Canvas/Remotion/HyperFrames]. Drive animation from an explicit time value so any frame can be rendered directly. Render stills at the start, each transition, and the end; inspect them and fix overlaps before exporting the video.
```
