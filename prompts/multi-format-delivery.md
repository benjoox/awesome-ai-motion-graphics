# Multi-format delivery

**Reference:** [MakerMap's specification](https://x.com/verbove/status/2103483957266268381). This is an original template.

~~~text
Adapt [source project] to [aspect ratios and resolutions] at [fps]. Preserve approved copy, story, music, and event timing.

Create layouts from the same timed sequence and inputs. Reposition and rescale type, UI, characters, and camera framing to preserve hierarchy and detail. Do not crop a finished wide video to obtain portrait.

Show representative compositions at each format's tightest layout and important transitions. Check safe areas, wrapping, cursor actions, image resolution, and reading time. Make composition changes without adding unsupported content.

Verify dimensions, frame count, duration, full decoding, and audio presence. Inspect playback and phone readability. For a requested loop, review the joined end and beginning with sound. Deliver named videos, source, render commands, and limitations.
~~~
