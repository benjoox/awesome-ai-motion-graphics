# Data story

**Reference:** [Glowing network clusters](https://claudevideo.org/videos/glowing-network-clusters-map-a-media-conversation) — visual reference; verify the underlying dataset separately.

```text
Make a [25]-second animated data story from [dataset path]. The question is [question], and the takeaway supported by the data is [takeaway]. Identify the columns, units, dates, and missing values before designing anything. Never fabricate data or animate values that are not in the source.

Create three beats: establish the baseline, reveal the change or comparison, and hold on the conclusion with the key numbers visible. Choose a chart form appropriate to the data, then explain why. Use motion to guide attention between actual values, not to imply extra precision. Put axes, units, and a source credit on screen.

Stage: [size]. Palette: [colors including contrast choice]. Type: [font]. Make the chart readable at phone size. Generate frames deterministically from the data and time value, and inspect the start, peak, and final hold. Deliver the project and video.
```
